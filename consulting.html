I help companies big and small identify a high-level story that powers success—in sales, marketing, fundraising, and recruiting – and create event content and materials that get results.

Most companies (and individuals too!) don’t have a marketing problem. They have a messaging problem. They know their business but not how to communicate its benefits effectively, saying what they want to say and not what their audience needs to hear. I help clients:

  • Define what they really are
  • Clarify what they need to say
  • Communicate it effectively across channels
  • And I advise events on top content and promotion

About Me

I’m a veteran marketer with extensive experience strategizing, creating, and delivering targeted messages to diverse global audiences. I’ve had senior marketing roles for some of the top financial firms in the world, including Morgan Stanley, AllianceBernstein, and Brown Brothers Harriman, and have advised startups and hedge funds on their marketing. I also branded, innovated, and executed content and promotion for the OurCrowd Global Investor Summit, the largest investor event in the Middle East.

My Position on AI

I use AI as a well-informed, eager (but occasionally clueless) assistant to produce useful research in real time and to generate ideas I might not have thought of otherwise. But four-year-olds can’t (yet) replicate the insight, individualization, and gut that only experience brings. No matter how good the boat, it still needs a captain.
